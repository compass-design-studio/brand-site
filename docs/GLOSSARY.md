# Glossary

The words we use for Brand resources and the Compass brand, so the site, the design system artifact and our conversations name things the same way. Definitions say what a thing *is*. Rules about how to use it live on the site and in the design system.

## The site and its sources

**Brand resources**:
The public website (brand.compass-design.studio) where people download Compass marks and read the brand rules. It is the published copy of the design system. It also hosts unlisted pages for clients ([ADR 0005](./adr/0005-unlisted-client-pages.md)).
_Avoid_: brand hub, brand page, brand kit, brand portal

**Design system**:
The master record of the Compass brand (tokens, marks, rules and copy), kept as a private artifact in our organization's claude.ai account. When it and Brand resources disagree, the design system wins.
_Avoid_: brand book, style guide, source of truth (say "design system")

## Visitors

**Employee**:
A person on the studio's staff. The main visitor to Brand resources.

**Contractor**:
Someone hired to do work, in either of two senses: a freelancer working for the studio (a drafter, designer, photographer or web developer), or a builder or trade working on a project. Context says which. A main visitor alongside employees.
_Avoid_: vendor

**Trade partner**:
A business working alongside the studio that puts the studio's name on something, such as a sign shop, printer or builder. A builder can be both a contractor and a trade partner. A secondary visitor today, expected to become the main one as the studio grows.
_Avoid_: partner (alone), vendor

**Client**:
A person or family the studio designs a home for. Clients visit only the unlisted pages made for them, through a link we send, and never use the marks.
_Avoid_: customer, homeowner

## Client pages

**Testimonial page**:
The unlisted page at brand.compass-design.studio/testimonial that asks a client for a selfie video, has them sign off on how we'll use it, and takes the upload. It isn't in the site menu, the sitemap or search engines.
_Avoid_: selfie video guide, UGC page

**Selfie video**:
A short video, about a minute, that a client records on their own phone about working with the studio, in their own words.
_Avoid_: UGC, selfie testimonial

**Sign-off**:
What a client agrees to before their selfie video uploads: we may share it on our website, social media and in ads, trimmed but never changed in meaning, named the way they chose. Each video keeps a record of the sign-off's exact wording and version.
_Avoid_: release, waiver, consent form

**Testimonial interview**:
A recorded video call with a client about working with the studio, booked separately. The testimonial page offers it only as a quiet link.

## Marks

**Mark**:
Any of the studio's logo artworks: a glyph, wordmark, lockup, tile or wordmark bar. The umbrella term.
_Avoid_: logo (for one specific version)

**Wordmark**:
COMPASS with the glyph standing in for the letter A, with no descriptor. Comes in all six colorways.
_Avoid_: alt wordmark, logotype

**Descriptor**:
The words "Design Studio" set in spaced capitals beside or under the wordmark in a lockup.
_Avoid_: tagline, strapline, subtitle

**Lockup**:
The wordmark together with the descriptor in a fixed arrangement: horizontal (on one line) or stacked (descriptor underneath).
_Avoid_: combomark, combination mark, logo with tagline

**Glyph**:
The compass-A on its own: a drafting compass drawn as the letter A.
_Avoid_: monogram, icon, emblem

**Tile**:
The glyph centered on a solid-color square. The website favicon is one tile in use, not a separate mark. The email tile is the same idea on a wide rectangle, for email signatures.
_Avoid_: emblem, badge, avatar, favicon (as a mark name)

**Wordmark bar**:
The wordmark centered on a solid-color bar.
_Avoid_: banner

**Colorway**:
One of the six color treatments a mark comes in. Two-tone, navy ink and gold deep are for light backgrounds (two-tone and gold deep on Paper and white only); reversed is for Ink; paper and light gold are for Ink and dark areas of photos. Two-tone means Navy ink letters with a Gold deep glyph; reversed means Paper letters with a Light gold glyph. Tiles and wordmark bars are named by their art color on their background color instead.
_Avoid_: variant, color version

## Using marks

**Background**:
The surface a mark sits on, described as light or dark, or by its color ("on light backgrounds", "on Ink").
_Avoid_: ground ("ink grounds" reads literally to a printer)

**Clear space**:
The empty margin around a mark that nothing may enter.
_Avoid_: padding, exclusion zone

**Minimum size**:
The smallest size a mark may be reproduced at before its fine lines close up.

**Usage rules**:
The rules for using the marks: contrast, colors, clear space, minimum size, photos, other companies' logos, and who may use and approve marks. The design system holds the wording, and Brand resources shows them visually.
_Avoid_: ground rules, logo guidelines

**Approved background**:
A background listed for a colorway because every color in the mark keeps at least 3:1 contrast on it.

## Colors

The design system holds the exact values. These are the names.

**Navy**:
The brand blue that carries weight: icons, links and the info state.

**Navy deep**:
A darker navy for headings and link text.

**Navy ink**:
The darkest navy, the color of the wordmark and glyph on light backgrounds.
_Avoid_: navy (when you mean the wordmark color)

**Ink**:
The near-black used for body text and dark backgrounds.
_Avoid_: black, charcoal

**Paper**:
The warm off-white page background.
_Avoid_: white, cream

**Paper 2**:
A slightly darker paper for alternate bands.

**Tan**:
The warm neutral for quiet panels and cards.

**Gold**:
The accent used like a fine pen line: rules, eyebrows, hovers. Never a fill for a main surface.

**Gold deep**:
A darker gold for the glyph and descriptor in two-color marks, and for gold text on paper.

**Light gold**:
The pale gold for accents on Ink, the square of the navy ink tile, and the light gold wordmark.
