# The design system artifact is the master; Brand resources is its published copy

The Compass Design System artifact on claude.ai is the master record of the brand: tokens, marks, rules and copy. Brand resources (this repo, served at brand.compass-design.studio) is the published copy for people who can't open our organization's claude.ai artifacts. This repo is the source for the site itself, but it never invents a brand rule. A brand change or correction goes into the artifact first, then comes here.

## Consequences

- In v1 the copy is kept in step by hand. A repeatable sync is planned in [issue #1](https://github.com/compass-design-studio/brand-site/issues/1).
- When this repo and the artifact disagree, the artifact wins, unless an ADR here records a deliberate page-level difference (see [0002](./0002-page-text-meets-wcag-aa.md)).
